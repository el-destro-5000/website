Privacy Policy

CHONK CRUNCH · EFFECTIVE 7 AUGUST 2026 · UPDATED 18 OCTOBER 2026

> THE_SHORT_VERSION

Chonk Crunch collects gameplay and crash information, linked to a random ID rather than to you, so we can fix bugs and understand which parts of the game people enjoy, and keeps records of in-app purchases so they can be delivered and protected.

We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or location. We do not sell or share your information, and there is no account to sign in to.

The game asks every player’s year of birth when it first starts. Children get no analytics, and in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom analytics only runs with your consent. You can turn analytics off at any time in Settings › Analytics, inside the game.

01

Who we are

Chonk Crunch is published by El Destro 5000. This policy covers the Chonk Crunch mobile game on iOS and Android (application identifier com.eldestro5000.chonkcrunch).

02

What we collect

Gameplay, crash and device information is collected through Google Firebase (Analytics and Crashlytics), which acts as our data processor. Purchase records are kept by our purchase-checking server, which also runs on Google Firebase.

GAMEPLAY EVENTS

A round started or ended, a score, a merge, a café unlocked, a tutorial step finished.

Why: to understand which parts of the game are enjoyed, too hard, or never seen.

AN ANALYTICS ID

Firebase’s app instance ID, collected only while analytics is on for you (see Turning analytics off).

Why: so events from one installation can be grouped together.

IN-APP PURCHASE RECORDS

The product, the store’s purchase token and a random install ID the app creates.

Why: to deliver and protect purchases, prevent fraud and meet legal obligations.

CRASH AND PERFORMANCE DATA

The point in the code where a crash happened, and diagnostic context.

Why: to find and fix crashes.

BASIC DEVICE AND APP INFORMATION

Device model, operating system version, app version, language.

Why: to reproduce bugs and know which devices to support.

Identifiers we use

Firebase’s app instance ID, collected by Google Analytics only while analytics is on for you — see Turning analytics off.

A random install ID created by the app, sent to our purchase-checking server when the app starts and with each purchase, to deliver and protect purchases.

Neither is an advertising ID — neither is your device’s advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA), and neither can be used to recognise you in other apps.

The game does have permission to read the Android advertising ID, but only our advertising partners use it, and only for players aged 13 or over — see Advertising below. It is never added to our analytics.

03

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, postal address, precise or approximate location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera input, or any payment information. (For players aged 13 or over, our advertising partners may estimate a general location from your IP address — see Advertising.)

This is enforced in the game’s own code, not only by policy: before any event is sent, it is checked against a list of prohibited fields — including name, email, phone, address, location, IP address and any advertising identifier. If an event contains one of those fields it is discarded and never sent.

04

Turning analytics off

Whether analytics runs depends on your age and where you are:

  • In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, analytics runs only if you are at or above your country’s age for consenting to online services (between 13 and 16, depending on the country) and you agree to it in Google’s consent form. You can change that choice at any time in Settings › Privacy options.
  • Elsewhere, analytics is on by default for players aged 13 or over (on Android, once Google’s consent check has confirmed that you are outside the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom).
  • Children under 13, and players who have not yet told us their year of birth, get no analytics anywhere.

We judge where you are from your device’s own settings — its SIM card, mobile network or region — and from Google’s consent check, not from your precise location.

Wherever it is on, you can switch it off at any time in Settings › Analytics. When analytics is off, events are discarded inside the game before they are recorded or queued — nothing is stored on your device waiting to be sent, and no analytics are transmitted. The setting takes effect immediately and persists between sessions.

Crash reporting stays on when analytics is off, so we can still fix crashes that would otherwise make the game unplayable — except for players in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom who are under their country’s consent age, or have not told us their age, from whom we collect no crash reports at all.

05

Deleting your data

You can ask us to delete the analytics data linked to your device at any time. In Chonk Crunch, open Settings, choose Delete my data, and hold the button for two seconds. We then ask Google to erase the Google Analytics history for your device, and the app switches to a new analytics ID that isn’t linked to the old history. Your game progress is stored only on your device and isn’t affected.

We keep records of in-app purchases (the product, the store’s purchase token and a random install ID the app creates) for fraud prevention and to meet legal obligations. Crash reports are deleted automatically after 90 days. To stop analytics collection altogether, turn off Settings › Analytics.

06

Information stored only on your device

Your game progress and settings — score, unlocked content, volume, haptics and your analytics choice — are saved locally on your device. They are not uploaded to us. Uninstalling the game removes them.

If you allow notifications, reminders are scheduled by your device itself. We do not send push messages from a server, and no notification data leaves your device.

If you use the share button to save a screenshot, the image is written to your photo library by your device’s own share sheet. The game does not read your photo library.

07

Who your information is shared with

We share the information described above with Google, who provide Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics and the hosting for our purchase-checking server on our behalf. See Firebase’s privacy information and Google’s privacy policy.

For players aged 13 or over, the advertising partners named under Advertising receive the information described there, which they use to provide ads.

We do not sell your information for money or share it with data brokers. Under some US state laws, letting advertising partners use your advertising ID for personalised ads can count as “selling” or “sharing” it. You can limit this with your device’s own ad controls — resetting or deleting your advertising ID, or opting out of personalised ads — described under Advertising. We may disclose information if required by law.

08

Advertising

Chonk Crunch can show ads: short videos you choose to watch for a reward, ads between rounds, and a banner. They are delivered through Unity LevelPlay, which selects an ad from our advertising partners — ironSource Ads, Unity Ads and Google AdMob.

To show, measure and limit ads, and to prevent ad fraud, these partners collect your device’s advertising identifier (on Android, the Android Advertising ID, and the app set ID), your IP address, from which they may estimate your general location, basic device and app information such as model, operating system and language, diagnostic information about how the ads perform, and how you interact with the ads. Where the law requires your consent — for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom — the game asks for it through Google’s consent form before any ad loads, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy options.

Players under 13, and players who have not told us their age, are never shown an ad: the ad software is never started for them, so neither it nor our partners receive their advertising identifier.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device’s settings (on most Android phones, under Privacy › Ads). Each partner’s own privacy policy explains what it does with this information: Unity (LevelPlay, ironSource Ads and Unity Ads) and Google AdMob.

09

Where information is processed, and how long we keep it

Firebase processes data on Google’s servers, which may be located outside your country. Analytics data is retained according to our Firebase configuration and is deleted automatically when that period elapses. Crash reports are deleted automatically after 90 days. Purchase records are kept for fraud prevention and to meet legal obligations.

Information is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).

10

Children

Chonk Crunch contains no chat or other communication features and no social features. When the game first starts it asks every player for their year of birth, without suggesting an answer, and keeps only that year, on the device (and in the device’s own backup, if you use one). It is never sent to us.

Players under 13, and players who have not answered, are treated as children:

  • They are never shown ads, and the ad software is never started for them, so neither it nor our partners receive their advertising identifier.
  • No analytics are collected from them.
  • Like every player, they send the random install ID described in What we collect to our purchase-checking server when the game starts, so that purchases can be delivered; if a purchase is made, its record is kept as described there.
  • Outside the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, crash reports are also collected. A crash report carries a random installation identifier created by Firebase Crashlytics, plus device and app details. It is used for one purpose only: to find and fix crashes so the game keeps working. It is never used for advertising, never used to build a profile of the player, and is not linked to any name or contact details, which we do not have. It is shared only with Google, as our processor.
  • In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, no crash reports are collected from them either. The same applies to players there who are under their country’s age for consenting to online services. (We judge where a player is from the device’s own settings, as described in Turning analytics off.)

How long we keep children’s information: crash reports are deleted automatically after 90 days. The year of birth stays on the device (and in its own backup, if you use one) until the game is uninstalled. Purchase records are kept for fraud prevention and to meet legal obligations, as for every player. We keep nothing else.

Because we do not have a child’s name or contact details, we cannot pick out a particular child’s crash reports to remove them early; they expire on their own after 90 days. If you are a parent or guardian with a question about your child’s information, please contact us using the details below.

Delete local data

Delete analytics data
Use Settings › Delete my data — see Deleting your data above.

Ask about collected data
Contact us using the details below.

Depending on where you live you may have additional rights over your personal information, such as access, correction, deletion or objection. Contact us and we will respond.
