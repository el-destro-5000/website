Chonk Crunch

Drop · Merge · Chonk

Cozy merge puzzle iOS & Android No timers Plays offline

Chonk Crunch is a cozy cat merge puzzle. Drop round, extremely fluffy cats into the jar and watch two of a kind squish together into something bigger and even fluffier. Simple to pick up, hard to put down, and completely relaxing — there is no timer, no failure music, and nothing chasing you.

Twelve tiers of increasingly magnificent chonk, from a tiny cherry-sized kitten all the way up to the legendary Cosmic Cat. Your score is not just a number: earn fish, yarn and catnip as you play, then spend them furnishing a warm little café — and eventually seven more places, because the cats have ambitions.

No account and no sign-up; your progress is saved on your device.
