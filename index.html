> THE_STUDIO

Two people, zero meetings,
one suspiciously ambitious idea.

El Destro 5000 is a two-person studio, which is a generous way of saying we don’t have a HR department to stop us. No publisher, no roadmap committee, no slide deck explaining our “synergies” — just one of us writing the code and the other making it look like something worth your evening.

We’re not shipping anything until it’s actually good, so this site is mostly a promise and a vibe right now. If that sounds like your kind of trouble, say hi at contact@eldestro5000.com.
