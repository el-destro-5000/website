Privacy Policy

KITTY CAB JAM · EFFECTIVE 16 OCTOBER 2026

> THE_SHORT_VERSION

Kitty Cab Jam is a free puzzle game. The first time you open it, we ask the year you were born. If you tell us you are under 13, or you choose not to say, the game shows no ads at all and the advertising services are never started.

We collect anonymous gameplay and crash information to fix bugs and improve the game. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or location, there is no account, and we do not sell your information. Your birth year stays on your device; we only ever record an age group.

Players aged 13 and over may choose to watch a reward ad for an extra parking bay. Ads only play when you tap to watch one, and where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK) a consent dialog appears first.

How to delete your data ›

01

Who we are

Kitty Cab Jam is published by El Destro 5000. This policy covers the Kitty Cab Jam mobile game on Android (application identifier com.eldestro5000.kittycabjam), distributed through Google Play. Our Terms of Service also apply.

02

The age question

On first launch the game asks “What year were you born?”. Nothing is pre-selected and you can choose “Prefer not to say”. You can play the whole game whatever you answer.

Your answer is stored only on your device. We never send the year anywhere; our analytics receive only an age group (under 13, 13–17 or 18+) so we know the answer was given.

Players who answer under 13, or who prefer not to say, are treated as children: no ads are shown, the advertising services are never started, and no advertising data is collected about them (see section 09).

03

What we collect

Analytics information is collected through Google Firebase Analytics and crash reports through Google Firebase Crashlytics; Google acts as our data processor for both. Our analytics do not collect your device’s advertising identifier, for any player.

GAMEPLAY EVENTS

A level started, won or lost, an extra parking bay unlocked, and the age group from the first-launch question.

Why: to understand which levels are enjoyed, too hard, or never reached.

AN ANONYMOUS INSTALL IDENTIFIER

A random value generated on your device the first time the game runs.

Why: so events from one installation can be grouped together. It is not linked to your name or any account.

CRASH AND PERFORMANCE DATA

Where in the code a crash or error happened, the app version, device model and operating-system version.

Why: to find and fix crashes.

BASIC DEVICE AND APP INFORMATION

Device model, operating system version, app version, language, and approximate country derived from your IP address.

Why: to reproduce bugs and know which devices and languages to support.

ADVERTISING DATA (PLAYERS 13 AND OVER ONLY)

Your device’s advertising identifier, IP address, coarse (city-level) location derived from it, device information, and which ads were shown and tapped.

Why: our advertising partners use this to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud. It is collected by those partners only when an ad is requested, only for players who answered 13 or over, and only after consent where the law requires it. See section 04.

04

Advertising

Players who answered 13 or over can choose to watch a short reward ad to unlock an extra parking bay. A reward ad only plays when you tap to watch one, and you never need to watch one to keep playing. The game does not show banner ads, and it does not show full-screen ads you did not choose; if that ever changes we will update this policy first.

Ads are delivered through Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) mediation, which requests ads from ironSource Ads, Google AdMob and Unity Ads. These networks receive the advertising data described in section 03 and act as independent controllers of it. See Unity’s privacy policy (Unity Ads and LevelPlay), ironSource’s ads privacy notice and how Google uses information from apps that use its services (AdMob).

For players under 13, or who preferred not to say, none of these advertising services is started, so they receive no information from the game at all.

Your choices about ads

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK), a consent dialog appears before any ad is requested. Everywhere, you can reset or delete your device’s advertising identifier in Android’s own Settings › Privacy › Ads, or simply never tap to watch an ad.

05

Purchases

Kitty Cab Jam currently has no in-app purchases. If we add them, they will be processed by Google Play (we never receive card details), and we will update this policy before they go live.

06

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, postal address, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera input, or any payment information. There is no account, no chat, no friends list and no way to share content with other players.

07

Information stored only on your device

Your progress and settings — levels cleared, sound, music and vibration settings, language, and your answer to the age question — are saved only on your device. There is no cloud save: if you uninstall the game or reset your device, that progress is gone. The game does not send push notifications.

08

Who your information is shared with

Google, who provide Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics on our behalf. See Firebase’s privacy information and Google’s privacy policy.

Unity (LevelPlay, ironSource Ads, Unity Ads) and Google (AdMob), for the advertising described in section 04 — players 13 and over only.

We do not sell your information or share it with data brokers. We may disclose information if required by law.

09

Children

Kitty Cab Jam is a cozy puzzle game that people of all ages may enjoy. To protect younger players, the first-launch age question decides whether advertising is ever switched on.

  • For players who answer under 13, or who choose “Prefer not to say”, the game shows no ads, never starts the advertising services, and does not collect or share the device’s advertising identifier.
  • For these players we collect only the anonymous gameplay, crash and device information in section 03, without any advertising identifier, and use it only to run, fix and improve the game — never for advertising, profiling or to contact the child.
  • We never ask a child for their name, email, phone number or location, and the game has no chat or social features.

If you are a parent or guardian and want to know what has been collected from your child, or want it deleted, contact us at the address below (see also Delete your data). We will respond and delete it.

10

Where information is processed, and how long we keep it

Our processors run on servers that may be located outside your country, including in the United States.

How long we keep it: analytics event data is deleted automatically after at most 14 months. Crash reports are deleted automatically by Firebase Crashlytics after 90 days. Ad-serving data (players 13 and over) is retained by the advertising partners under their own policies.

Information is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).

11

Your choices and rights

Control ads
Use the consent dialog where shown, reset your advertising identifier in Android settings, or never tap to watch an ad.

Delete local data
Uninstall the game to remove everything stored on your device, including your age answer and progress.

Delete your data

To ask what we hold or to have collected data deleted, email contact@eldestro5000.com with the subject “Kitty Cab Jam data request”. Because we do not collect identifying information, please include your device model and the approximate dates you played so we can locate the relevant records; in some cases we may not be able to identify data as yours. We respond within 30 days. Analytics and crash data are also deleted automatically on the schedule in section 10.

Depending on where you live you may have additional rights over your personal information, such as access, correction, deletion or objection. Contact us and we will respond.

Legal bases (EEA and UK)

We rely on your consent for personalised advertising (given or refused in the consent dialog), and on our legitimate interests in keeping the game working, secure and free of fraud, and in understanding how it is played, for analytics and crash reports. You can object to processing based on legitimate interests by contacting us, and you may complain to your local data-protection authority.

12

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. Material changes — for example adding purchases, a new kind of ad, or a change in the data our partners receive — will be described here before they take effect.
