Buffalo & Co. Gold Rush Solitaire is TriPeaks solitaire, played in landscape on a gold-rush trail map. Your guide is the buffalo showman himself, now riding as the Trail Boss in a cattleman hat, a duster and a red neckerchief. Tap any open card one higher or one lower than the top card to clear it, keep the run going, and clean out the board — that’s a Strike!

The Trail runs 160 claims across 8 territories, and the ground changes under you as you go: Classic boards, Twin Peaks, Mesa and Sawtooth Ridge. Further down the trail the Horseshoe turns up — a wild card waiting on the board.

Fairness is the whole point. Every claim is played through by a solver and fair-play bots before it ships, and only boards verified winnable make it onto the Trail. No rigged deals, no secret difficulty dials, and no real-money wagering anywhere — the gold is for playing, not gambling.

Gold nuggets are the currency out here. Swing by the Daily Dig every day for free nuggets, then spend them at the Trading Post on new card backs. Free to play, with optional in-app purchases (nugget packs) and ads.
