Privacy Policy

SKYWARP · EFFECTIVE [EFFECTIVE DATE]

> THE_SHORT_VERSION

Skywarp is a free simulated slot-machine game for adults, supported by optional reward ads and in-app purchases. The coins in the game are virtual currency with no real-world value — nothing you win can be cashed out, and nothing about money or gambling accounts is collected, because there are none.

We collect anonymous gameplay and crash information to fix bugs and improve the game, and our advertising partners use your device’s advertising identifier to show and measure reward ads — with a consent dialog first where the law requires one.

We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or precise location. There is no account to sign in to: your progress lives on your device. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play — we never see your card details. We do not sell your information.

You can erase everything we hold from inside the game with Settings › Delete my data, or by emailing us. Details in section 07.

01

Who we are

Skywarp (a working title — the game may ship under a different name, and this policy will follow it) is published by El Destro 5000, a studio based in the United States. This policy covers the Skywarp mobile game on Android (application identifier com.eldestro5000.skywarp), distributed through Google Play.

The game is offered to players aged 18 and over in the countries where it is listed on Google Play. It is not offered in Washington State. Our Terms of Service explain the rules of play, including that the coins are virtual currency with no monetary value.

For the purposes of data-protection law, El Destro 5000 is the controller of the information described in this policy, except where we say a partner acts as an independent controller.

02

What we collect

Analytics and crash information is collected through Google Firebase (Analytics and Crashlytics), which acts as our data processor. We do not run our own servers at launch, and there is no account, so nothing we collect is tied to your name.

GAMEPLAY EVENTS

A spin played, a bonus feature triggered, a machine unlocked, a daily bonus claimed, virtual coins earned or spent inside the game, a reward ad watched, a purchase completed.

Why: to understand which parts of the game are enjoyed, confusing, or never seen, and to balance the game. Coin amounts are virtual game currency — not money, and not payment data.

AN ANONYMOUS INSTALL IDENTIFIER

A random value generated on your device the first time the game runs.

Why: so events from one installation can be grouped together. It is not derived from your name, email, phone number or Google account. Deleting your data (section 07) or uninstalling the game discards it.

CRASH AND PERFORMANCE DATA

The point in the code where a crash happened, and diagnostic context such as free memory and what screen was open.

Why: to find and fix crashes.

BASIC DEVICE AND APP INFORMATION

Device model, operating system version, app version, language, screen size, and the country your Google Play store is set to.

Why: to reproduce bugs, know which devices to support, and, for players in the United States, apply the Washington State exclusion (see section 01).

ADVERTISING DATA (REWARD ADS ONLY)

Your device’s advertising identifier, IP address, coarse (city-level) location derived from it, device and app information, and which ads were shown, watched to completion, and tapped.

Why: our advertising partners use this to serve relevant ads, prevent fraud, and measure ad performance. See section 03 for who receives it and the choices you have.

PURCHASE CONFIRMATIONS

A Google Play purchase token and order confirmation for each in-app purchase, and which virtual item it unlocked.

Why: to deliver what you bought, verify the purchase was genuine, and handle refund reversals. It contains no card number, name or billing address.

03

Advertising

Skywarp shows reward ads only — short videos that play only when you choose to watch one in exchange for a virtual reward such as bonus coins. There are no banner ads and no interstitials, and no ad ever starts on its own.

Ads are delivered through Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) mediation, with Google AdMob as a participating ad network. The ad networks participating in an ad request receive the advertising data described in section 02 and act as independent controllers of it. See Unity’s privacy policy, ironSource’s ads privacy notice, and how Google uses information in advertising.

Your choices about ads

Where the law requires it (for example in the United Kingdom, the European Economic Area, and certain US states), a consent dialog appears before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice later from Settings › Privacy options inside the game. Everywhere, you can reset or delete your device’s advertising identifier in Android’s own Settings › Privacy › Ads; if you delete it, ads become non-personalised.

Because reward ads only ever play when you tap to watch one, the simplest way to keep ad partners from receiving anything at all is not to watch them. You never have to watch an ad to keep playing.

04

Purchases

In-app purchases — packs of virtual coins and other virtual items — are processed by Google Play. Google handles your payment details; we never receive your card number or billing address — only confirmation that a purchase completed, so we can deliver what you bought.

The coins and items you buy are virtual currency and virtual goods with no monetary value. They cannot be redeemed for money, transferred to another person, or cashed out, and buying them gives you a limited licence to use them inside the game, as described in our Terms of Service. Refunds follow Google Play’s refund policy; we do not handle refunds ourselves.

There are no subscriptions in Skywarp.

05

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, postal address, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera input, biometric data, or any payment information. We do not collect your date of birth — the age gate on first launch only records that you confirmed you are 18 or over. There is no account, no chat, no friends list and no social feature.

Because there is no real money in play, we hold no bank details, no wallet and no balance that could be paid out.

06

Information stored only on your device

Your game progress and settings — virtual coin balance, unlocked machines, level, daily-bonus timers, statistics, volume, motion and haptics preferences, your age-gate confirmation, and your responsible-play settings (spend limits and self-exclusion, see section 08) — are saved locally on your device. They are not uploaded to an account, because there is none. Uninstalling the game removes them, and your virtual coin balance with them.

Cloud save may come later. At launch there is no cloud save. If we add an optional cloud-save or account feature in a future update, it will be opt-in, this policy will be updated before it goes live, and the update will describe exactly what is stored and where.

07

Deleting your data

There are two ways to have your data deleted. Both are free, and neither requires an account.

In the game

Open Settings › Delete my data and confirm. The game erases all locally stored progress and settings (including your virtual coin balance, which cannot be restored), discards the anonymous install identifier, and sends a deletion request for the analytics and crash records linked to that identifier. The game then restarts as a fresh install. Reward-ad data held by advertising partners is deleted through their own processes (see section 03) and by resetting your advertising identifier in Android settings.

By email

Write to contact@eldestro5000.com with the subject line Skywarp data deletion. Because we hold no identifying information, please include your device model and the approximate dates you played so we can locate the relevant records; where we cannot link records to you, we will tell you so. We respond within 30 days, and sooner where the law requires it.

Purchase records held by Google Play are governed by Google’s own policies and are not deleted by either method; you can manage them from your Google account.

08

Responsible-play settings

Skywarp is a game of chance played for entertainment with virtual currency, and we want it to stay fun. In Settings › Play responsibly you can set a spend limit on in-app purchases (daily, weekly or monthly) and a self-exclusion period that locks the game for the time you choose. These settings are stored only on your device; we do not receive them and do not use them for advertising. How they work, and how to request an exclusion from us directly, is described in our Terms of Service.

Unity (ironSource) and the ad networks in our mediation stack, for the reward advertising described in section 03.

We do not sell your information or share it with data brokers. We may disclose information if required by law, to enforce our Terms of Service, or to protect the rights and safety of players and others. If El Destro 5000 is ever acquired or merges with another studio, the information described here may transfer to the new owner, who will be bound by this policy.

We do not sell personal information for money. Under California law and some other US state laws, sending advertising data to ad partners to show personalised ads can count as “sharing” or “targeted advertising”. You can opt out at any time using the choices in section 03; if you opt out, ads you choose to watch will be non-personalised.

10

Where information is processed, and how long we keep it

Our processors run on servers that may be located outside your country, including in the United States. Where information about players in the United Kingdom or the European Economic Area is transferred abroad, our processors rely on standard contractual clauses or equivalent safeguards under their own terms.

Analytics data is retained according to our Firebase configuration and is deleted automatically when that period elapses. Crash reports are retained while they remain useful for fixing the crash. Purchase confirmations are retained for as long as needed to deliver and support the purchase and to meet tax and accounting rules. Ad-serving data is retained by the advertising partners under their own policies.

Information is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).

Children
